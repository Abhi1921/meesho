Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my order on Meesho? ▼
You can track your order directly on Meesho by visiting your Profile -> Orders section on meesho.com or the Meesho mobile app.
What is Meesho's Return and Refund Policy? ▼
Meesho offers a 7-day hassle-free easy return policy for most products. Refunds are initiated instantly upon product pickup.
Does Meesho support Cash on Delivery (COD)? ▼
Yes! Cash on Delivery is available for all products listed across India with zero extra fees.
